Connecting Algebra, Graphs & Reality

Why Do Exponential & Logarithmic Functions Matter?

Exponential functions model phenomena that grow or decay by a constant percentage per unit time (finance, biology, physics). Logarithmic functions solve for unknown time/rates in those models and create human-readable scales for quantities spanning many orders of magnitude (earthquakes, acidity, sound).

Why Asymptote N = 0 Matters:

Each half-life cuts the remaining substance in half, approaching zero asymptotically without mathematically reaching zero.

Scientists find the age of fossils using the inverse log: t = −ln(N / N0) / k.

Why Use a Logarithm Here?

Seismic waves range from 102 to 109 times baseline intensity—too huge for a linear scale!

Using log10 compresses factors of 10 into steps of +1:
I2 / I1 = 107.0 − 5.0 = 102.0 = 100
