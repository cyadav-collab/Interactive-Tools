Student Inquiry Task

15-Minute Asymptote Investigation

Explore the interactive graph on the right to answer these 4 quick mystery questions before your teacher introduces rational functions!

Task 1: The Broken Graph Mystery 3 mins

Set the sliders on the right to a = 2, b = 4, c = 1, d = -2. Look at x = 2 on the graph. Why does the curve split into two infinitely tall branches here?

Denominator calculation: (1)x + (-2)
When x = 2: (1)(2) - 2 = 0
Key Insight: What happens when you divide any number by zero in mathematics? This vertical "forbidden wall" is called a Vertical Asymptote.
Task 2: The Long-Distance Ceiling 4 mins

Keep a = 2, b = 4, c = 1, d = -2. Look at what happens to the graph when x becomes very large (like x = 8 or 10). What y-value does the graph level off towards?

Task 3: The Ratio Pattern 4 mins

Change parameter a to 4 and c to 2. Notice where the horizontal leveling line goes. Now try a = 6, c = 2. What is the pattern between coefficients (a, c) and the horizontal asymptote y?

Rule Discovered: As x grows huge, the terms b and d become negligible! The graph approaches the simple ratio y = a / c.
Task 4: Real-World Business Test 4 mins

A business spends 1200 dollars on equipment plus 15 dollars per item made. Average cost formula is:
Average Cost = (1200 + 15x) / x

Can the business ever sell hoodies for an average cost of 10 dollars if raw materials cost 15 dollars per hoodie?

Sandbox Function Graph

f(x) = (2x + 4) / (1x + -2)
Live Coordinates Tracker: Move sliders to see how the undefined line moves!

Function Form

f(x) = (ax + b) / (cx + d)

IB Key Properties

Vertical Asymptote: x = 2.00

Horizontal Asymptote: y = 2.00

x-intercept: (-2.00, 0)

y-intercept: (0, -2.00)

Domain: x in R, x ≠ 2.00

💡 IB Teaching Tip: Point out how f(x) = (ax + b) / (cx + d) can be rewritten as f(x) = k + A / (x - h) through division, revealing horizontal translation h and vertical translation k.
Classroom Hook Scenario

The Hoodies Startup

A group of IB students start a business selling custom hoodies. They invest in printing equipment and digital assets, incurring fixed overhead costs plus production costs per hoodie.

Average Cost Model Formula:

Average Cost C̄(x) = (1200 + 15x) / x = 15 + 1200/x

Notice how as x becomes very large (x → ∞), the average cost C̄(x) approaches 15 dollars. The line y = 15 forms the horizontal asymptote!

Medicine & Science Connection

Drug Concentration in Blood

When a patient takes medication, the concentration C(t) in mg/L in their bloodstream t hours after ingestion can be modeled by a rational function.

Concentration Function:

C(t) = 6t / (t² + 4)

  • Initial state (t = 0): C(0) = 0 mg/L
  • Peak time: t = 2 hours (C = 1.5 mg/L)
  • Long-term (t → ∞): C(t) → 0 mg/L (Horizontal Asymptote y = 0)
IB Reflection Prompt: Why is the horizontal asymptote y = 0 biologically necessary for this model? What would happen to the patient if y = 2?
